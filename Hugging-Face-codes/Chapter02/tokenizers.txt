. Why do we need a tokenizer?

LLMs cannot directly understand text. They process numbers.

"I love cricket"
       ↓
   Tokenizer
       ↓
["I", "love", "cricket"]
       ↓
[40, 2321, 10543]
       ↓
     Model

A tokenizer converts text → tokens → token IDs.


2. Three main tokenization approaches

Word-based

"I love cricket"
→ ["I", "love", "cricket"]
Simple
Huge vocabulary
Lots of unknown words ([UNK])
Doesn't naturally capture relationships like run / running

Character-based

"cat" → ["c", "a", "t"]
Tiny vocabulary
Almost no unknown tokens
But creates many tokens, making processing expensive

Subword ⭐

"tokenization"
→ ["token", "ization"]

This is the important one for modern LLMs.

It gives a balance between vocabulary size, meaningful units, and sequence length.


| Algorithm                   | Example                         |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **BPE / Byte-level BPE**    | GPT-2 and many GPT-style models |
| **WordPiece**               | BERT                            |
| **SentencePiece / Unigram** | Many multilingual models        |
