A pipeline has three major steps

Input text
    ↓
1. Preprocessing
    ↓
2. Model inference
    ↓
3. Postprocessing
    ↓
Final result

============================================================

For example:

from transformers import pipeline

classifier = pipeline("sentiment-analysis")

classifier("I love this!")

==============================================================

Underneath, HF is essentially doing:

"I love this!"
      ↓
   Tokenizer
      ↓
Token IDs + Attention Mask
      ↓
Transformer Model
      ↓
Model Head
      ↓
Logits
      ↓
Softmax
      ↓
POSITIVE: 0.99

================================================================

Transformers cannot directly understand raw text.

The tokenizer:

Text
 ↓
Tokens
 ↓
Token IDs
 ↓
Model inputs
==================================================================
from transformers import AutoTokenizer

tokenizer = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(checkpoint)

Tokenizer converts human language into numerical inputs that the Transformer can process
